## Why these articles are here

These articles are not about the abstract “world of Rust.” They explore real engineering problems that arise in a project such as `RustShot`:

- coordinates versus pixels
- overlay and multi-monitor behavior
- video recording and the fragility of the Windows media stack
- installers, automatic startup, and release builds

Possible future topics include:

- testing desktop applications
- utility UX without a large interface
- WinAPI and Rust
- turning a personal utility into a polished product
