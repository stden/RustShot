# Release as a Product

## Canonical source

- [Release as a Product: Installers, Pipelines, and Delivery Discipline](../../articles/004-release-is-a-feature.ru.md)

## Retrieval summary

- A working executable is not a complete product when installation, autorun, uninstall, or published artifacts fail.
- Repeatable releases require the correct target, predictable artifact names, aligned versions, installer construction, and publication of both portable and installed forms.
- Delivery quality affects how safely and frequently the project can ship fixes.
- The essay supplies the rationale; the release concept page links the current installer and workflow contracts.
